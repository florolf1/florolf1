# Hi, I’m Florian 👋

**Civil engineer who writes software.** I survey buildings — and write the software for them.

Self-employed software developer since 2016, Member of the Executive Board at [TwinOne AG](https://twinone-ag.de) since 2026.
My focus is the path in between: from laser scan to BIM model to the software people work with day to day.

🌐 [www.florianwinter.com](https://www.florianwinter.com/en/) · ✉️ [mail@florianwinter.com](mailto:mail@florianwinter.com)

## What I work on

- **Surveying** — 3D laser scanning and surveys of existing buildings.
- **Software for construction** — BIM, scan-to-BIM, AR/VR/XR for the construction site and platforms like [TwinEstate](https://twin-estate.com).
- **AI for construction and real estate** — AI assistant in TwinEstate, TwinAI at TwinOne AG and AI co-workers built from agent teams.
- **Crypto and blockchain development** — validator infrastructure, Web3 platforms and interfaces for decentralized exchanges.
- **iOS apps** — 20 apps for iPhone and iPad since 2016, over 5M App Store downloads.
